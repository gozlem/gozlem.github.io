Ece Mutlu completed her Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering and Management Systems at UCF in Summer 2022. Her dissertation, "Complex Quantum Contagion: A Quantum-Like Approach for the Analysis of Co-Evolutionary Dynamics of Social Contagion," applied quantum-inspired methods to model and analyze social contagion processes.
