Ozlem Ozmen Garibay is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Systems at the University of Central Florida. Her research spans AI applied to bioinformatics (drug discovery, molecular design), responsible AI (fairness, interpretability, human-centered design, bias mitigation), and social cybersecurity (AI models to understand and counter influence operations on social and traditional media). She directs research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, complex systems, and network science, with applications in STEM education analytics and higher education economic engagement.

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, University of Central Florida, 2002-2008. Dissertation: "Analyzing the effects of modularity on search spaces."
- **M.Sc. in Computer Science**, University of Central Florida, 2000-2001
- **B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering**, Middle East Technical University (METU), 1993-1998
