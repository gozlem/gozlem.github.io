Amirarsalan Rajabi completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science at UCF in Fall 2022. His dissertation, "Addressing Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence: Fair Data Generation and Classification, and Analysing Algorithmic Curation in Social Media," explored fairness in AI systems and the role of algorithmic curation on social media platforms.
