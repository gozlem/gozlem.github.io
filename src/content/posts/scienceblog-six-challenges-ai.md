Our paper **"Six human-centered artificial intelligence grand challenges"** was featured on [ScienceBlog](https://scienceblog.com/researchers-identify-6-challenges-humans-face-with-artificial-intelligence/), bringing our research to a broad science audience.

The article covers our 20-month study involving 26 international experts from academia, industry, and government across North America, Europe, and Asia. The team identified six core challenges that must be addressed for the development of safe and trustworthy AI:

1. **Human Well-Being** — AI systems should actively seek opportunities to enhance human welfare
2. **Responsible AI** — Prioritizing societal well-being throughout the AI lifecycle while mitigating ethical risks
3. **Privacy** — Safeguarding individual data collection and preventing harmful misuse
4. **Design** — Implementing human-centered frameworks that categorize AI risk levels appropriately
5. **Governance and Oversight** — Creating frameworks spanning conception through deployment
6. **Human-AI Interaction** — Maintaining human control and responsibility while respecting cognitive capacities

*"These challenges call for the creation of human-centered artificial intelligence technologies that prioritize ethicality, fairness and the enhancement of human well-being."*

The study was published in the *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction* (DOI: [10.1080/10447318.2022.2153320](https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2022.2153320)).
