Dr. Ozlem Ozmen Garibay and Aida Tayebi from the University of Central Florida were featured contributors in the [Drug Target Review Screening eBook 2022](https://www.drugtargetreview.com/article/106963/drug-target-review-screening-ebook-2022/). The eBook covers how **artificial intelligence can identify drug candidates** and data-led screening models, bringing together experts from academia and industry including researchers from Aria Pharmaceuticals and the Rosalind Franklin Institute.
