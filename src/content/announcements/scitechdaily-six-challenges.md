Our paper **"Six human-centered artificial intelligence grand challenges"**, published in the *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction*, was featured in [SciTechDaily](https://scitechdaily.com/6-challenges-identified-by-scientists-that-humans-face-with-artificial-intelligence/). The article highlights the six key challenges identified by our team that humans face with artificial intelligence, emphasizing the need for human-centered approaches in AI development and deployment.
