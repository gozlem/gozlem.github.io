Our research was covered by [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/984019), the leading science news service operated by AAAS, in a press release titled **"Researchers identify 6 challenges humans face with artificial intelligence."**

Dr. Ozlem Garibay spearheaded the investigation involving **26 international experts** from North America, Europe, and Asia representing academia, industry, and government. The 20-month study identified six grand challenges for human-centered AI: human well-being, responsible AI, privacy, design, governance and oversight, and human-AI interaction.

UCF co-authors include Gavriel Salvendy, Waldemar Karwowski, Steve Fiore, Ivan Garibay, and Joe Kider. The study was published in the *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction* (DOI: 10.1080/10447318.2022.2153320).
