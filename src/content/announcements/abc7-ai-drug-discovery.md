Our research was featured on [ABC7 News](https://abc7.com/post/artificial-intelligence-attentionsitedti-ai-in-health-field-fda-approved-drugs/13614086/) in a segment titled **"From the lab to the pharmacy: How AI is speeding up the discovery of new drugs."**

The report highlights our **AttentionSiteDTI** technology — an AI-based screening approach that analyzes drug-protein interactions by converting complex molecular interactions into language patterns, predicting binding behavior with **97% accuracy**. The tool can test compounds against all FDA-approved drugs, dramatically reducing the typical 10-year drug development timeline.

Dr. Ozlem Garibay noted: *"I think it's going to revolutionize medical fields in so many different ways."* The research is a collaboration with materials science professor Sudipta Seal and doctoral researcher Mehdi Yazdani-Jahromi at the University of Central Florida. AttentionSiteDTI is currently available for researchers at no cost, with plans to create an accessible platform for broader adoption.
