Dr. Ozlem Garibay was featured in [UCF Pegasus Magazine](https://www.ucf.edu/pegasus/advanced-medicine/) for her research on **the potential of AI in healthcare**. The article highlights how her team is using artificial intelligence to transform pharmaceutical development and disease diagnosis.

Traditional drug discovery methods require 10–20 years to match compounds with viruses through trial and error. Dr. Garibay's AI-driven approach accelerates this process by screening suitable drug candidates more rapidly and efficiently, with the potential to significantly reduce the over $20 billion in annual costs.

The research, supported by a **$40,000 UCF research grant**, is conducted in collaboration with engineering professor Sudipta Seal at UCF Research Park. The project originated during the early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic and has since expanded to broader applications in disease prevention — identifying biological markers before symptoms appear to enable personalized healthcare.
