Our research on challenges in AI was featured in [Bug](https://www.bug.hr/umjetna-inteligencija/sest-kljucnih-izazova-s-kojima-se-moramo-suociti-u-primjeni-ui-32612), a Croatian technology magazine, in an article titled **"Šest ključnih izazova s kojima se moramo suočiti u primjeni UI"** (Six key challenges we must face in AI application).

The article covers the six critical challenges identified by our research team that society needs to address as artificial intelligence becomes more widely adopted.
