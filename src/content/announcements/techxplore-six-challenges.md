Our research was featured in [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2023-03-humans-artificial-intelligence.html) in an article titled **"Researchers identify 6 challenges humans face with artificial intelligence."**

The 20-month research effort brought together a team of **27 researchers** — international experts from academia, industry, and government spanning computer science, psychology, medicine, and engineering from North America, Europe, and Asia. The study identified six core challenges for human-centered AI:

1. **Human Well-Being** — AI systems should enhance user welfare during interactions
2. **Responsible AI** — Prioritizing societal benefit and human values alignment throughout AI lifecycles
3. **Privacy** — Protecting individuals from harmful data misuse
4. **Design** — Creating risk-assessment frameworks for AI deployment decisions
5. **Governance & Oversight** — Establishing lifecycle management from conception through deployment
6. **Human-AI Interaction** — Maintaining human control and cognitive respect in system design

The findings were published in the *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction* (DOI: 10.1080/10447318.2022.2153320).
