Our research was featured in [UCF Today](https://www.ucf.edu/news/ucf-researchers-are-advancing-ai-assisted-drug-discovery/) in an article titled **"UCF Researchers Are Advancing AI-assisted Drug Discovery."**

The piece highlights how our team at the University of Central Florida is leveraging artificial intelligence to accelerate the drug discovery process, building on our **AttentionSiteDTI** framework that predicts drug-protein binding interactions with high accuracy.
