Our research was featured in [UCF News](https://www.ucf.edu/news/researchers-identify-6-challenges-humans-face-with-artificial-intelligence/) highlighting the publication of **"Six Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Grand Challenges"** in the *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction*.

Dr. Ozlem Garibay led the 20-month study with 26 international experts from academia, industry, and government across North America, Europe, and Asia. The team identified six core challenges for developing trustworthy AI: human well-being, responsible AI, privacy, design, governance and oversight, and human-AI interaction.

*"These challenges call for the creation of human-centered artificial intelligence technologies that prioritize ethicality, fairness and the enhancement of human well-being,"* Garibay stated.

Contributing UCF faculty include Gavriel Salvendy, Waldemar Karwowski, Steve Fiore, Ivan Garibay, and Joe Kider.
